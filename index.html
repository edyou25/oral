双人口语练习

给物业打电话报修

AI 出题

你们要用英语描述空调漏水的问题，并约好维修时间。

A
租客。空调昨晚开始漏水。周三上午你有组会，下午 3 点后在家。
B
物业。问漏水从什么时候开始。周三上午有空档，下午还有一个 4 点的空档。

先脱稿聊一轮。交换角色后，把周三改成周五，再聊一轮。

初始答案 · AI 模拟

AHi, my air conditioner is leaking. It start last night.

BIs it still leaking now? Can the repair person come Wednesday morning?

AYes. I have a meeting in the morning. I can at home after three. Can change to afternoon?

BYes, we have four o’clock. Is that okay?

AYes, four is okay. Thank you.

优化后的答案 · AI 示例

AHi, I’m calling about my air conditioner. It started leaking last night.

BIs it still leaking? Could someone come by on Wednesday morning?

AYes, it is. I have a meeting that morning, but I’ll be home after three. Could we do Wednesday afternoon instead?

BSure. We have a slot at four. Does that work for you?

AFour works for me. Thanks!
