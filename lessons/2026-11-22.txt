【场景】
物业来电，周三下午 4 点的维修要改期。用英语重新约时间。

【角色】
物业。原来的时间取消了。周四上午 10 点或下午 5 点半可以上门。
租客。周四上午你有实验，下午 5 点后可以。问清楚师傅大概几点到。

【初始对话】
Hi, we need change your repair time. Can you do Thursday at ten?
I have experiment in the morning. I can after five.
We can come at five thirty. Is okay?
Yes. What time he will arrive?
Around five thirty. We call you before.

【优化对话】
Hi, we need to reschedule your repair. Does Thursday at ten work for you?
I have an experiment that morning, but I’ll be home after five. Could we do Thursday evening instead?
Sure. We have a slot at five thirty. Does that work for you?
That works. What time should I expect the repair person?
Around five thirty. We’ll call before we arrive.

【笔记】
We need to reschedule your repair.
用 reschedule 表示改期。

What time should I expect the repair person?
用来问维修师傅大概几点到。
